Straight answers.

What is bukz?

A memecoin on Solana with one job: the creator fees from its trading buy real in-game vehicles and properties into a single public game account that the shop controls. The showroom lists what it buys, the garage shows what it owns, the ledger shows every step.

How do fees become cars?

Fees are claimed from pump.fun in SOL, sold for dollars, spent on official in-game cash cards from the publisher's own store, which land as in-game cash in the shop account. The account buys the next item in the showroom order. Receipts and screenshots go on the ledger.

Do holders get the cars?

No. The items stay in the shop's account. They are not backing for the token, they are never sold for holders, and they are never handed out. The point is a garage everyone can see filling up.

Who controls the account?

The shop's team. The account name goes on the ledger the day the first purchase is made, with screenshots. The ledger is a plain JSON file anyone can read.

What happens on November 19?

The new game opens. If the new city has purchases, the garage moves there on day one and the showroom list is replaced with the new city's prices. If it doesn't, the shop keeps buying in the current game. Either way the ledger continues.

Is this affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. bukz is an independent fan project. Cash cards are bought from the official store at the published price like any other customer. All game names and marks belong to their owners.

Is this advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
