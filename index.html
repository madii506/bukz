The shelf

The official cash card lineup. Pick one, type a handle, send the order. The shop confirms on X before you pay anything.

What you need
  • A GTA handle. The recipient's online name, exactly as it appears in the game.
  • Their platform. PC, PlayStation or Xbox. Codes are platform-specific.
  • An X handle for delivery. The code is sent by DM, so DMs from the shop must be open.
  • SOL. You pay after the shop confirms the amount. Never before.
What the shop does
  • Replies to your order on X with the total in SOL and where to send it.
  • Buys the official code for the right platform once the SOL arrives.
  • DMs the code to the X handle you gave. Usually the same day.
  • Logs the order on the ledger: date, card, handle, proof. Never the code.

Delivery depends on what each platform allows for codes in the recipient's region; the shop says so before you pay. Official cash cards at the official price plus the shop fee.

Talk to the shop

Straight answers.

What is bukz?

A shop and a coin. The shop sells official in-game cash cards for GTA and delivers them to any player's handle. The coin, $BUKZ on Solana, pays a creator fee on every trade, and the shop spends those fees on drops: cards gifted to GTA handles. Every order and every drop is logged on a public ledger.

How do I gift cash to someone?

Open the shelf, pick a card, type the recipient's GTA handle and platform and an X handle for delivery, then send the order. The shop replies on X with the SOL amount. You pay, the shop buys the official code for that platform and DMs it. The order goes on the ledger with a receipt.

Can I send it to myself?

Yes. Your own handle, your own X account.

What does a drop mean?

A card bought with the coin's fee money and gifted to a GTA handle for free. Holders who registered their handle are in every draw. The community can nominate handles too. Drops are gifts, not a return, not a dividend, and not a promise.

Where do the fees go, exactly?

Fees are claimed from pump.fun in SOL, sold for dollars, and spent on cards for drops. Every claim is a line on the ledger with the SOL amount; every drop is a line with the card, the handle and the proof. If it isn't on the ledger, it didn't happen.

Which platforms?

PC, PlayStation and Xbox. Codes are platform-specific and some platforms restrict codes by region. The shop tells you what's possible before you pay.

What happens on November 19?

The new city opens. The moment it sells in-game cash, the shelf switches to the new city's cards. Same shop, same ledger. If that takes a while after launch, the shop keeps selling the current game's cash until then.

Is this affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. bukz is an independent fan project. Cash cards are bought from official stores at the published price and delivered as official codes. The shop is a buyer and a courier, nothing more. All game names and marks belong to their owners.

Is this advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Nothing on this site is financial advice.
